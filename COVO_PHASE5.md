# Covo Phase 5 — Detailed 3D motion preview

Launch `dist/app/covo-3d.html` from a local HTTP server. Requires Three.js from a CDN.

Adds reference-inspired gold hair ornaments, animated braids, blue glowing spiral markings, outfit sash with secondary motion, wrist cuffs and boot trim. Adds luminous traveling projectile halos and expanding ground impact rings to the existing ranged palm/foot attacks. Preserves the 80×80 aerial free-movement arena and iPhone controls.

**Limitations:** This remains a procedural articulated model, not a reference-accurate sculpted/skinned GLB, and is a standalone lab rather than integrated multiplayer. Braids and sash use procedural secondary motion, not physics simulation. No iPhone Safari validation performed.

Play directly at `/app/covo-3d.html` after deployment.
