# PocketMonstars original creature art

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final project assets are in `dist/art/` as cindercub.png, mossprout.png, ripplefin.png, voltwing.png, duskhorn.png, pebblit.png, frostuft.png, bloomtail.png, and soloryx.png.

Prompt set: original full-body transparent collectible monster game illustrations, smooth hand-painted anime treatment, dark contours, cel shading, intelligent faces, combat-ready three-quarter poses facing right, full tails/feet/horns visible. Friendly creature appeal with evolved fantasy anatomy and natural armor. No pixel art, text, background, logos or existing franchise characters.

Species briefs:
- Cindercub: copper ember lynx, cream chest, obsidian leg armor, amber forehead rune, flame ears and smoke tail.
- Mossprout: green forest armadillo-stag, ivory bark armor, fern antlers, jade eyes, leaf ears and spiral tail.
- Ripplefin: teal lagoon otter-drake, silver forearm fins, sapphire gem, webbed paws and water-fin ribbon tail.
- Voltwing: gold storm falcon-griffin, black crest, amber wings, graphite armor, cyan eyes and lightning feathers.
- Duskhorn: violet twilight ram-panther, crescent horns, silver armor, lilac eyes, white mane and crystal tail shards.
- Pebblit: slate pangolin-rhino, ochre crystals, chisel horn, emerald eyes and armored tail.
- Frostuft: snow fox-owl, crystal brows, feathered forelegs, azure ears, feather tail and silver claws.
- Bloomtail: rose forest hare-kirin, petal ears, jade horn, golden eyes and three blossoming tails.
- Soloryx: gold celestial oryx-lion, branching antlers, indigo markings, pearl armor, sun gem and solar mane.

The game renders these images with smooth filtering, gentle idle motion, palette variation and shadows. Distinct local DNA IDs are not a guarantee that artwork cannot repeat globally; production uniqueness must be enforced separately.
